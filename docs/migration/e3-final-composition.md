## Qualifications implementation must preserve or explicitly correct

- An automation's idle/completed receipt is not verification that its intended work succeeded. Workspace creation or terminal launch can precede a later failure.
- Account/environment preparation may have partial effects before generation. A WSL UNC home on a host-target environment path yields success without an environment, not the refusal suggested by earlier shorthand.
- `files.commitUpload` may publish a destination before cleanup rejects. Its SSH wrapper does not establish the local exclusive-copy guarantee.
- Updater results join platform ownership, not integrations. An installation request is not evidence of restart or health.
- Notification replay is bounded to 256 retained entries; identical watermarks do not guarantee identical results after that buffer changes.
- Mentu's RPC host coercion and optional-parameter normalization differ from direct IPC. Run records are neither formal Commitment Protocol records nor process-liveness evidence.

Each source defect requires separate characterization and intended-correction tests. Do not silently encode a defect as the desired product guarantee.
